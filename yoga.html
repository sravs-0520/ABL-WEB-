Meditation techniques

New to meditation? Start with any of these. No experience needed.

🌬️

Breath focus

Sit comfortably and follow each breath in and out. When your mind wanders, gently return.

5 minutes
🫶

Body scan

Move your attention slowly from head to toe, noticing and softening each part.

15 minutes
🔔

Sound meditation

Listen to singing bowls and let the sound carry your thoughts away.

20 minutes
